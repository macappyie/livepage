Advance Course Curriculum

Day 1 — Trading Performance Audit
  • Analysing the last 100–200 trades
  • Win rate
  • Average win / average loss
  • Expectancy
  • Profit factor
  • Maximum drawdown
  • Best/worst setup
Day 2 — Finding Your Real Trading Problem
  • Overtrading
  • Revenge trading
  • Early entry
  • Late entry
  • Premature exit
  • SL shifting
  • Averaging losers
Day 3 — Expectancy & Probability
  • Win rate ≠ profitable strategy
  • R-multiple
  • Expectancy
  • Risk of ruin
  • Losing streak calculation
Day 4 — Drawdown Management
  • Daily loss limit
  • Weekly loss limit
  • Position reduction after losses
  • Recovery mathematics
Day 5 — Position Sizing
  • Capital-based sizing
  • SL-based quantity
  • Fixed fractional risk
  • Volatility-based sizing
Day 6 — Trading Journal Deep Dive
  • Setup-wise statistics
  • Time-wise performance
  • Stock-wise performance
  • Long vs short performance
  • Market-condition performance
Day 7 — Performance Review

Day 8 — Market Regime

Divide the market into 4 conditions:

  • Trending bullish
  • Trending bearish
  • Range-bound
  • High-volatility

Important: the same strategy will not work in every market condition.

Day 9 — Setup Classification

Dividing the student's trades into categories:

  • Breakout
  • Pullback
  • Reversal
  • Momentum
  • Mean reversion
Day 10 — Setup Quality Scoring

Score every trade: A+ / A / B / C setup. Factors:

  • Trend
  • Structure
  • Volume
  • VWAP
  • Relative strength
  • Volatility
  • Confirmation
Day 11 — Entry Optimization

Within the same setup:

  • Early entry
  • Confirmation entry
  • Retest entry

Historical comparison of all three.

Day 12 — Exit Optimization
  • Fixed target
  • Trailing SL
  • Partial booking
  • Structure-based exit
  • Time-based exit
Day 13 — Stop Loss Engineering

Not a random ₹ amount for SL:

  • Structure SL
  • ATR-based SL
  • Candle-based SL
  • Volatility-adjusted SL
Day 14 — Build One Personal Strategy

Helping the student finalize 1 strategy.

Day 15 — Market Internals
  • NIFTY structure
  • Sector strength
  • Market breadth
  • Leading/lagging sectors
  • Relative strength
Day 16 — Institutional Activity
  • FII/DII data
  • Futures positioning
  • OI
  • Price + OI relationship
Day 17 — Advanced Option Chain
  • OI concentration
  • OI change
  • Volume
  • PCR
  • IV
  • Strike-wise positioning
Day 18 — Option Greeks Practically

Not just definitions:

  • Delta → directional exposure
  • Theta → time decay
  • Vega → IV exposure
  • Gamma → Delta sensitivity
Day 19 — IV & Volatility
  • IV Rank
  • IV percentile
  • IV expansion
  • IV contraction
  • IV crush
Day 20 — Option Buying vs Selling

When option buying can be mathematically difficult, and when defined-risk structures can be used instead.

Day 21 — Weekly Live Case Study

Analysing one complete market day in the format: pre-market → entry → management → exit → post-market review.

This is the part that sets your course apart from normal coaching.

Day 22 — Backtesting

Testing 100–500 historical trades of the student's strategy. Calculate:

  • Win %
  • Average R
  • Expectancy
  • Profit factor
  • Max DD
  • Losing streak
  • Recovery period
Day 23 — Overfitting

Very important: modifying a strategy so heavily to fit past data that historical results look excellent, but it fails in the future.

  • Over-optimization
  • Curve fitting
  • Data snooping
Day 24 — Walk-Forward Testing

Backtest → Out-of-sample → Forward test

Day 25 — Trade Distribution

Example: how actual profitability develops from +3R, +2R, +1R, 0R, -1R across 100 trades.

Day 26 — Monte Carlo / Probability

Understanding by randomizing the historical strategy:

  • Possible drawdown
  • Losing streak
  • Expected equity curve
  • Risk of ruin
Day 27 — Execution Analysis

More important than brokerage:

  • Slippage
  • Spread
  • Liquidity
  • Market orders
  • Limit orders
  • Entry delay
  • Exit delay
Day 28 — Trading Psychology with Data

Not just “maintain discipline.” Identify from the journal:

Loss → emotional trade → overtrading → bigger loss — and quantifying it.

Day 29 — Build Personal Trading Playbook

The student's own document: Market Condition → Setup → Entry → Confirmation → SL → Target → Position Size → Exit → No-Trade Condition

Day 30 — Final Trader Assessment

Give the student live/historical market data. They must decide for themselves: Trade / No Trade

Then review their decision statistically.
