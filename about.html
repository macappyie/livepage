Who We Are

Structured market education, built on discipline

Money Assure is a stock market education company based in Navi Mumbai. Our courses take learners from market fundamentals through technical analysis and options trading, using a structured curriculum and live, mentor-led instruction.

Our role is purely educational. We do not offer stock tips or manage client funds, and we make no promises of returns. Our focus is on clear teaching, guided practice and consistent mentorship, so every student can develop the skills to make independent, well-informed decisions.

Meet Your Mentor

The person you'll actually learn from

MP
Mukesh Shalan Mahadev Patil

Founder & Mentor, Money Assure

BE, LLB (Mumbai University)

My journey into the markets didn’t start with a straight path — like most traders, it started with curiosity and a few expensive lessons. Early trades taught me what no course ever could: that the market punishes impatience and rewards discipline. Those initial losses became the foundation of how I trade and teach today — with a strong focus on risk management, patience, and process over prediction.

Over the years, I’ve built hands-on experience across technical analysis, fundamental analysis, and options trading — not from theory, but from real capital, real mistakes, and real recovery. That’s also why Money Assure exists: to help students skip the expensive trial-and-error phase and learn the fundamentals the right way, from someone who’s actually been through it.

Today, alongside 2–3 dedicated faculty members who share the same approach, we make sure every student gets the same direct, unfiltered attention I wished I’d had when I was starting out.
